## Overview
The Bill of Materials (BOM) lists the components needed to build the speaker subsystem. It includes the quantity, cost, manufacturer, supplier, datasheet, and schematic reference for each component. The BOM is used to organize the parts needed for the project and estimate the total cost of building the board.
